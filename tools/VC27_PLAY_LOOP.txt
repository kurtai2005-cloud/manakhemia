Spatial forage on the current Main and the 675 garden. Not the finished product.
A headless run is not a phone or GPU picture.

Rule, from the existing specs
- data/hunting.json names the fruit. This loop uses the existing starberry picture and CollectionSystem fruit count. It does not add a currency.
- Each walk home has a delivery number. That number pays one fruit once. The same number pays nothing a second time, including after a reload. The berry comes back for the next number.
- data/sim.json care.feed_meal is 40 hunger and food_mood_bonus_warm is 3. One stored fruit calls Sim.feed("meal"). feed_refuse_at 90, an egg, or sleep spends nothing.
- Personality daily_shift on eat_together moves the social axis by its existing delta, inside the existing daily cap. That is the bond.
- data/sim.json sick_threshold is 15. Online growth_sec moves only while the pet is not unwell. The meal does not add growth time. It raises hunger so the existing clock can move.
- The first meal from a gathered fruit makes the home marker solid. Rest is allowed below sleep_min_energy_to_sleep (90). Below 50, rest wins over another meal and also starts on its own. The pet walks to the home marker, enter_house, and Sim.set_sleeping. Energy uses sleep_regen_per_hour and wakes at sleep_wake_at, then exit_house.
- Offline time stays on the existing cap and does not finish a delivery or add this fruit. A backwards clock is ignored.
- Shop furniture in data/economy.json has prices. This loop does not buy it and does not grant a free shop item.

What you can do
- Click the berry. The pet walks, carries it, and walks to the home marker.
- Click the berry again to cancel. Click again to retry the same delivery.
- Click the home marker to walk there and eat one stored fruit. The coat plays eat, then the pet wanders.
- After that meal, click the home marker while tired. The pet walks home, goes inside, sleeps, and comes back out awake.
- Feed, pat, and clean on the pet still use the one coat.

Not in this slice
- More than one kind of forage, a shop, a second creature, or combat.
- A growth stage that jumps because of the meal itself.
- Room art, lighting, or a new picture.
- A phone or GPU result. Windows still has to look at the head.
